# Follower sprite audit (v0.4)

Roster: national dex #1-386 plus every species Shenron can grant (the v0.3 roster).
Data source: `src/data/pokemon/species_info/gen_*_families.h` in pokeemerald-expansion 1.17.1.
A missing overworld sprite falls back to the engine's Substitute doll (`OW_SUBSTITUTE_PLACEHOLDER`).

**Base species:** 455 entries, 455 with their own overworld sprite, 455 of those with a shiny overworld palette.
**Alternate forms (non-battle):** 93 entries, 78 with their own overworld sprite, 78 of those with a shiny overworld palette.
**Battle-only forms (never follow):** 76 entries, 47 with their own overworld sprite, 47 of those with a shiny overworld palette.
